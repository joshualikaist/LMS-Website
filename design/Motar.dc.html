01 / 03
PROBLEM
Why is chasing a moving target harder than ordinary navigation?
P-01
THE TARGET HIDES
Every time the drone swerves around an obstacle, it loses sight of the target — and has to keep flying on memory.
P-02
THE GOAL MOVES
There is no fixed destination. The point to reach is somewhere the target will be — not where it is now.
P-03
THE SPACE IS CROWDED
In a dense obstacle field, the safe path and the intercepting path are rarely the same path.
02 / 03
APPROACH
See, decide, fly — with nothing but onboard sensors.
A-01
SEE
A camera and LiDAR give the drone its own view of the scene — no external tracking, no prebuilt map.
A-02
DECIDE
A learned policy turns that view into the next maneuver, trained in simulation across millions of attempts.
A-03
FLY
A flight controller keeps every maneuver within what the aircraft can physically do.
